- **Focus**: Responsiveness, accessibility, and interactive elements.

## 📝 Content Strategist
- **Responsibility**: Brand voice, product descriptions, and SEO optimization.
- **Focus**: Compelling copy that reflects the premium nature of Whole Melts Extracts.
