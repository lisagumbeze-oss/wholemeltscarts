# Solventless Gold

A luxurious, high-end cannabis atelier theme. Obsidian depth layered with burnished gold accents and warm ivory typography.

## Color Palette

- **Obsidian Black**: `#050505` - Primary deep background and foundational depth
- **Burnished Gold**: `#D4AF37` - Signature accent for highlights, buttons, and text gradients
- **Warm Ivory**: `#F3F0E8` - Primary text color for high legibility with a premium feel
- **Muted Resin Green**: `#3D9B6E` - Secondary accent for success states and organic touches

## Typography

- **Headers**: Playfair Display
- **Body Text**: Manrope

## Best Used For

Premium cannabis brands, luxury extract storefronts, high-end distillery sites, and connoisseur lifestyle products.
